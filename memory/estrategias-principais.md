# Operating playbook

Read this before a piece, a week, or a traffic plan. The long guides hold the why. This file is what to run.

Read the brand first. Niche, audience, promise, goal, pillars, and what the brand never does live in `estrategia/01-posicionamento-e-pilares.md`. Voice lives in `memory/estilo-de-escrita/guia-voz.md`. Platforms live in `memory/social_media_handles.md`. Tool choices live in `memory/config.md`.

Detail: copy in `estrategia/10-copywriting.md`, distribution in `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`, offer in `memory/diretrizes-hormozi.md`, ranking notes in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`.

## What the goal changes

`Goal` in `estrategia/01` is one of growth, leads, sales, or authority.

- **Growth or authority, and no paid offer yet.** Publish. The bottleneck is how often finished pieces ship. A larger backlog does not replace a publish date.
- **Leads.** Same publishing floor, plus one lead magnet from `freebies/README.md`. The exchange is a way to reach the person again. The social account stays rented land.
- **Sales.** The parked sections in the traffic and offer guides turn on. Cold traffic still gets the problem, the proof, and a low-friction next step. The sale sits on the destination page and in the list.

## Three truths while reach is the job

1. **Frequency is the bottleneck.** A week spent preparing a bank, with nothing published, extends the phase where the account has no watch history. Set a start date. Keep the buffer to about a week of pieces.
2. **The piece is the targeting.** Short-form distribution reads the picture, the line, and what the first viewers do. Rewriting the hook is the test. Interest stacks and lookalikes are a later, paid concern.
3. **The owned list is the asset.** A short video opens the relationship. The caption, the carousel, or the long post carries the lesson. The profile, the newsletter, and the lead magnet are what the creator still has if a platform changes the reach. See `memory/content-format-cascade.md`.

## On every piece

1. **Hook inside the first sentence.** On-screen text, because most people watch with the sound off. One short sentence. One of the four families in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`. No specialist term that the next block has to explain (`memory/feedback_hook_writing_no_jargon.md`). Write several versions. The pipeline asks for three before the body.
2. **The old way failed the viewer, not the viewer.** Name the stuck method, then name this creator's way of solving it. The name comes from the promise in `estrategia/01`, not from a generic "tips" label.
3. **Proof is a real file.** A screen recording, a photo, a number from the creator's own work, a before and after they can show. The creator supplies the file. Anticipate the "this is too clean" reaction at the moment the proof appears.
4. **Use the audience's words.** Mine comments, replies, and public threads in the niche from `estrategia/01`. Keep the phrasing. A cleaned-up version sounds like a brochure.
5. **Teach inside a scene.** A dry definition loses to the moment the problem showed up, then the fix.
6. **Each line pulls the next.** Short lines. Varied length. Slide 1 of a carousel does most of the work. Slide 2 has to pay off the swipe. Build rules: `estrategia/03-templates-sops-calendario.md`.
7. **One idea, several native formats.** Mark the atoms first (`memory/content-format-cascade.md`). A derivative with no new angle does not ship. Do not drop two competing derivatives on the same platform the same day.
8. **One call to action.** A save, a send, or the comment word already stored in `memory/dm-automation.md` when `dm_automation` is `yes`. Do not invent a sample word. Do not stack follow, like, and comment.
9. **Voice wins over a clever line.** `memory/estilo-de-escrita/guia-voz.md` overrides this playbook on person, length, punctuation, and words the creator avoids.

## Distribution

1. **A hundred minutes a day on the primary action, for a hundred days,** while the goal is growth or authority. For this system the primary action is making and publishing. See `memory/diretrizes-hormozi.md`.
2. **Floor, not a wish.** 3–4 short videos and 1–2 carousels a week, plus a story on a day that has a real behind-the-scenes moment. The 30-day version is `estrategia/09-sprint-cold-start-30-dias.md`. If four videos would hollow out the dense edit, ship three. Do not thin the cut to hit a count.
3. **Two ways to make a video, chosen by the subject.** Dense is the default when the lesson is the point. Light is for opinion, reaction, and behind the scenes, where density is not the point. This template has no built-in light series. One appears later only through the feedback loop (`memory/feedback-repo-do-dia-format.md`).
4. **Iterate the winner.** Take the short video with the best hold. Change the hook. Publish again. Fatigue shows up in the opening before it shows up in the subject.
5. **Rewrite per platform.** Same file can be a Reel and a Short. The caption and the framing are rewritten. An identical paste is a miss.
6. **Read the metric that matches the failure.** Weak open (hook rate under the account's own average, or a drop in the first seconds) means the first sentence. A strong open and a weak hold means the middle is stalling. Saves and sends are the value signal. Likes are the weak signal. Definitions and thresholds: `estrategia/04-metricas-e-otimizacao.md` and `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`.
7. **About twenty minutes a day** in genuine comments on a short list of accounts in the niche, and a reply to comments on the new post inside the first two hours.
8. **Paid media waits.** Three to five organic pieces that already beat the account's average, a destination page whose first screen repeats the promise, and a low-friction next step. Then one campaign, a broad audience, budget at campaign level, no edits for about seven days. Detail: `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`.

## Offer layer that already applies

1. **A lead magnet solves one narrow, complete slice** of the problem in `estrategia/01`. It is good enough that someone would pay for it. It reveals the next problem. It does not pretend to be the whole result. Mold: `freebies/README.md`.
2. **Raise the felt odds and cut the time.** Show the result. Hand over the checklist, the template, or the steps they can run today.
3. **Name the piece with a magnet, a person, a result, a time box, and a container word** (guide, challenge, blueprint). Test the name without reshooting the lesson.
4. **Write to the first people who reply.** That is voice-of-customer research and a two-way conversation. It is not a blast.
5. **More, then better, then new.** More published pieces. Then a better hook, read from the metrics. A new platform or a new series only after those two.
6. **A sequence of attention, even with nothing for sale.** The free piece, then the list, then the place they can keep following. When a paid offer exists, the money-model section in `memory/diretrizes-hormozi.md` replaces this sketch. It does not run before that.

## Leave these alone until a paid offer exists

- Paid spend whose goal is followers.
- Many campaigns, narrow interest stacks, and a heavy attribution product.
- Guarantees, upsells, downsells, price anchors, and affiliates.
- A hard sell in the language of someone who already trusts the creator, pointed at someone who does not.

Copying the look of a viral video is also out. Take the verbal shape of an opener into `referencia/swipe-openers.md`. Keep the visual system in `estrategia/00-identidade-visual.md`.

## If the week only fits three moves

1. Publish at the floor.
2. Open with a specific hook and put real proof on screen.
3. If the goal is leads or sales, have one lead magnet that captures a way back to the person. If the goal is growth or authority, skip the magnet and protect the publishing floor.
