# Image and screenshot search is proactive

Whenever the agent suggests an `imagem` or `imagem+motion` for a cut note or an overlay step, it searches right then. It does not wait to be asked.

- **What the creator sees.** The options (screenshots of the real page plus images from the search) arrive on the review page for the creator to choose. The creator may upload their own. Use 6 to 8 numbered options plus a recommendation (`memory/formato-lista.md`).
- **Tool.** The contract is in `ferramentas/buscar-imagens/README.md`: a wide search, a download of every result, duplicates dropped, `origem.tsv` with file, query, and URL, then numbered contact sheets. Build the script from that README when a second piece needs it. Do not bake a brand's type or palette into it (`memory/visual_identity_standard.md`).
- **A still image alone usually fails.** Always suggest it with motion on top.
- **Editor package.** It carries the chosen images and `origem.tsv`.
- **Check the sheet.** A screenshot of a real page often catches a cookie banner. Look at the sheet and discard those.
