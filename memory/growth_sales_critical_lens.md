## How to apply

Before locking a piece or a week, check it against the notes below and against `estrategia/01-posicionamento-e-pilares.md`. A clash with the goal, the pillars, or the "never do" section gets named in the chat.

## Ranking signals (directional, collected 2026-08-12 and 2026-09-02)

Reported from Adam Mosseri's public comments, as repeated in secondary write-ups. Feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore rank separately.

1. **Watch time, including replays.** The strongest signal on short video. A short piece watched more than once can beat a longer piece watched once.
2. **Sends per reach.** The strongest signal for reaching people who do not follow. Secondary write-ups put it several times above a like. In the 2026-09 extension, shares were described as a top signal beyond Reels alone.
3. **Likes per reach.** Still a signal. Weaker than watch time and sends.
4. **Two-way conversation.** The 2026-09 collection reported that accounts the creator actually talks with (replies, story replies, direct messages) were getting an explicit boost from around March 2026. Reply like a person.
5. **Raw distribution versus a pure engagement rate.** A piece with high reach and a modest rate can still be the one to learn from. Do not kill a format on rate alone.
6. **On Reels specifically:** watch time, replays, shares, whether the audio matches how people are using sound, and skips. Sends remain the heavy distribution signal.

The same collection said the platform was preferring human footage over synthetic people in 2026. That supports showing the creator when `face` in `memory/config.md` is `yes`. It is not a reason to ignore `face: no`.

## Hook

The practical writing rule is `memory/feedback_hook_writing_no_jargon.md`: one short sentence, specific pain or curiosity, no unexplained jargon. The 2026-08 note put the real stay-or-swipe window near 1.5 seconds, shorter than the "first 3 seconds" shorthand. Plan the on-screen line so it can be read at a glance. Most viewers have the sound off, so the spoken line does not replace the text.

Four families:

- **Curiosity.** Opens a gap the viewer can feel.
- **Pain.** Names a frustration they already have, in their words.
- **Bold promise.** Leads with the result.
- **Story.** Makes them feel seen in the first line.

A vague hook fails. Pull the specific from the niche and the audience in `estrategia/01`. A definition that resolves itself in the same sentence fails even when the words are ordinary.

## Carousel

Directional, same collection: carousels tend to earn more saves than a single image, especially on teaching pieces. Slide 1 does most of the work. A useful length is about 7–10 slides. The build rules, including "obvious beats clever," live in `estrategia/03-templates-sops-calendario.md`. The caption is 1 to 3 sentences (`memory/feedback-carousel-pipeline.md`).

A carousel the viewer does not finish can be shown again later as unseen slides. A short video does not get that second chance. That is a reason to keep carousels in the week, not a reason to skip the short video. The short video is still the base format (`memory/content-format-cascade.md`).

## Two layers of pillars

Thematic pillars live in `estrategia/01` (three to six). A second layer, by job, sits across them:

- **Authority.** The creator teaches a framework.
- **Connection.** Values, a true story, real behind the scenes.
- **Proof.** A result the creator can show. A number from the work, not a borrowed stat.

About 80 percent of pieces serve the audience. Direct promotion stays near or under 20 percent, and lower while the goal is growth or authority. When a new idea is only self-promotion, say so and move it.

## Cold start

The hard phase is a small account with little watch history. Distribution by interest does not, by itself, punish a small account for being small. The missing history still does.

Recommended floor, including in that phase: **3–4 short videos and 1–2 carousels a week**, plus stories once there is a real moment to show. Waiting for a full bank extends the hard phase. Say that tension when the creator wants to pause. A fixed start date and a buffer of about one week is the mitigation. The execution plan is `estrategia/09-sprint-cold-start-30-dias.md`.

About twenty minutes a day of genuine comments on a short list of accounts in the niche helps the account get recognized. It does not replace publishing.

## Persuasion, applied

Seven levers: reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity.

- **Authority** is the teaching, tied to the pillars in `estrategia/01`.
- **Social proof** is a real result the creator can show.
- **Commitment** is one small ask (save, send, or the comment word when DM automation is on).
- **Unity** is "people in this situation," using the audience description in `estrategia/01`.
- **Reciprocity** is the lead magnet and the free teaching.
- **Scarcity and a hard guarantee** wait until there is a paid offer. Inventing a deadline with nothing behind it trains distrust.
