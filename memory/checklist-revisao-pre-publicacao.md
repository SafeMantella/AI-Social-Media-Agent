# Pre-publish review checklist (Reels and carousels)

Source: outside best-practice notes and a creative-team quality rubric, checked against the originals and filtered to what this system already decided. It complements the copywriting guide (copy approval) and `feedback-carousel-pipeline.md`. Publishing still requires the creator's approval.

## Reels and Shorts

1. **Hook in the first 2 seconds** (picture and speech). No slow open and no intro.
2. **Caption: the first 125 characters work alone** as the hook, before the "more" cutoff. Run `ferramentas/checar-legenda/checar-legenda <file> --platform <name>` for the character limit and the hashtag count. Do not count by hand.
3. **Loop:** the end connects to the beginning (the same word, a match cut, a sentence that joins). Replays and completion rate are the strongest signals.
4. **Hashtags: 5 to 15 relevant ones** on Instagram (not 30 generic tags). On Shorts, 3 to 5, including `#Shorts`.
5. **One call to action only** (the comment bait), not "follow plus like plus comment".
6. **Preview before upload:** watch the final file (resolution, text cut off by the interface, captions) and only then ask the creator to approve. A successful preview is not permission to publish.
6b. **Paid work:** the native paid-partnership label is on, and the advertising disclosure the country requires is visible before "more". A collab invite does not replace the label. See `memory/parcerias.md`.
6c. **A Reel reposted as a Short:** upload private, wait for the rights check, then publish. See `memory/youtube-shorts-repost.md`.

6d. **Claims check on the final text.** Rerun `memory/checagem-de-afirmacoes.md` on the version the creator edited. An edit can reintroduce an error, and the caption must not contradict the speech.

## Carousel

7. Alternate the background color between slides so the piece has rhythm. A strong title plus supporting text on every slide.
8. **Density: follow the creator's preference** (short, dry text; see `feedback-carousel-pipeline.md`). An outside rule of 40 to 80 words per slide **was not adopted**. It contradicts the edits the creator actually makes.

## Copy and script

9. **Three candidate hooks before the body**, each with a different trigger and structure (question, contrary claim, number, story, direct benefit, pattern break, an opponent), plus one line on why that hook. This is already step 3 of the script pipeline. Use the types as a menu.
10. Apply the vetoes in `estrategia/10-copywriting.md` (the Vetoes section), and read the piece out loud.

## Score rubric (0 to 10) for an important piece

Clarity (can be summarized in up to 5 words), hook (specific curiosity plus a concrete promise), voice, visual density (every element earns its weight), call to action (specific, single, minimum friction), and format fit (it looks native). **Minimum to publish: 6 on every axis, and nothing under 4.** A grave failure (0 to 3): a generic hook ("look at this", "did you know"), no call to action, or an inconsistent voice.

## Research and planning (two small gains)

- **A primary source is required:** at least one primary source (real comments, a thread, a forum the audience uses), and separate fact from opinion, on top of dated sources. This fits `/pesquisar`. It does not replace `last30days`. An item is verified only after its own content is opened, including a stretch from the beginning, the middle, and the end. A title, a thumbnail, a duration, or a view count is not proof. What was not opened is marked unverified.
- **Plan backward:** start from the publish date, list the deliverables, mark the critical path, and have a backup for the riskiest step (recording, approval, render).
