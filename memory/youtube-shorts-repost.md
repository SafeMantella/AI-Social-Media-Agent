# Reposting a Reel as a YouTube Short

Checked against YouTube's own help in 2026-10. Search again when this note is older than two or three months before you rely on it.

- Upload the Short as private. Wait for the rights check. Publish only after that check finishes.
- A Short longer than 60 seconds that gets a rights claim stays blocked.
- Replace the claimed stretch. Do not replace the whole video because one stretch was claimed.
