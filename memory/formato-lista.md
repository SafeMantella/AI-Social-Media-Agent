# List pieces

These rules apply when the piece is a list. Measures below came from one copy. Re-measure before treating a number as fixed.

## Order is a retention choice

Explain the order in one line per position before building.

- The first two items stop someone who is scrolling.
- The middle alternates mood.
- A strong item sits near the end.

## Review before approval (lists and comparisons)

Run this on any ranked list or A-versus-B script before the creator approves it. Say what you changed and why.

1. **Headline.** It carries a number or a promise, not only a label. If the topic was already a video, the new mechanic goes in the headline.
2. **Tease the last item.** The second sentence points at the final item without naming it, so a viewer stays to the end.
3. **Escalate.** Each pair or step is stronger than the one before. If a pair is two facts side by side, with no climb, reorder or rewrite it.
4. **Known to obscure.** Within a pair, the item most people know comes first.
5. **Weakest pair in the middle.** Never glue the weakest pair to the call to action, which is where a viewer decides whether to stay.
6. **Where the ask sits.** Keep the creator's follow ask where their edit recipe puts it (`memory/edicao-observada.md`), after the hook that earned it.
7. **Risky joke.** Flag a joke or comparison that lands on real victims or real people. Offer a replacement and leave the decision to the creator.
8. **Claims.** Run `memory/checagem-de-afirmacoes.md`.

## Do not repeat an item

Before selecting, read the earlier list pieces in the store. An item already used goes in a reserve, not in the new list.

## Official image

Each item needs its official image. An item without one goes to a reserve. Tell the creator before the assembly.

## Choosing images

Search widely, then send one numbered sheet of 6 to 8 options and the recommendation. The creator picks by number. The tool contract is `ferramentas/buscar-imagens/README.md`.
