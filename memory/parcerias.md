# Paid partnerships and the media kit

## Label

A paid post, a trade, or a partnership carries the platform's native paid-partnership label, plus the advertising disclosure the country requires. That disclosure is visible before "more". A collab invite does not replace the label. The pre-publish checklist includes this.

## Media kit

One HTML page with the real profile numbers, to send to brands and agencies. Numbers are exact. Do not round them. Do not invent a number that was not read off the profile.

## First message to an agency

Send it only after the media kit and the reply email exist. Send it to the company's profile, never to an employee's personal account.

At most 60 words: who the creator is, one or two real numbers, and an offer rather than a request. The ask is easy to answer ("what is the best email for the media kit?"). Never name a client, an event, or a gift as the ask.

Weekdays, two or three a day, and the wording changes. One reminder in the same conversation after 5 to 7 business days, then stop.

After an invite: confirm on time, keep the social embargo and the review embargo separate, and send the coverage numbers.
