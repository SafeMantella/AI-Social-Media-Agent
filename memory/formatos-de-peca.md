## List showcase

A short take with no speech. The creator points at the headline. Then fixed-length cards. Then an urgency end card. The trending music is added in the app, not in the render.

0.9 seconds per card was too fast. The range that held was 1.5 to 2 seconds. The end card was about 2.7 seconds. List rules: `memory/formato-lista.md`. Assembly contract: `ferramentas/montar-lista/README.md`.

## Two scenes per item

Three frames per item, from `ferramentas/montar-lista/README.md`. The title sits in the center. When the music is the same music as the reference, the duration of each position comes from that reference, measured cut by cut.
