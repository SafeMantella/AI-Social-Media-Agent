# Metrics and optimization

What to record, where, and how a number changes the next week. Ranking weights live in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`. Traffic thresholds live in `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`. Both decay. Re-check them before treating a benchmark as current.

Follower count is a result. Do not steer the week by it alone.

## 1. Where the numbers live

- `storage: notion` — one row per published piece in the Analytics database described in `estrategia/02-arquitetura-notion.md`.
- `storage: md` — the same row in `conteudo/metricas.md`.

Fill the row in the weekly review. Do not wait for a monthly essay.

## 2. What to record per piece

| Field | Why it is here |
|---|---|
| Date, platform, pillar | So a month can be compared with the mix in `estrategia/01-posicionamento-e-pilares.md` |
| Funnel stage | From `estrategia/07-funil-de-conteudo.md` |
| Views or reach, and reach beyond followers | Whether distribution left the existing audience |
| Watch time, replays, or completion | The heavy short-video signal |
| Saves | Teaching value |
| Sends | The signal that reaches non-followers |
| Comments worth a reply | Two-way conversation, not a raw count of emojis |
| Follows from the piece, when the platform shows it | Which pillar turns a viewer into a follower |
| One sentence of learning | Even "I do not know why" is a row the next review can read |

Likes can sit in the row. They do not win an argument against saves, sends, or watch time.

### How to read a platform

- **Short video (Instagram, TikTok, Shorts).** Completion and the first seconds. A weak open is a hook problem. A strong open and a weak finish is a stall in the middle. Sends and saves beat likes.
- **Carousel.** Saves and the swipe from slide 1. The caption is short on purpose. Do not judge a carousel by the same completion curve as a video.
- **A longer video.** Thumbnail and title click-through, average view duration, and whether the video delivered what the title promised. Subscribers gained on that video are the authority result.
- **A piece whose job is a tool or a community, not reach.** Also record the outcome outside the social dashboard: stars, replies from strangers, people who used the thing. That outcome does not get forced into a views contest.

Directional bands from outside roundups (completion, click-through, carousel versus video) go stale. Use them as a hint only until this account has a month of its own rows. Prefer the account's average after that. The 2026-08 and 2026-09 collection is dated in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`.

## 3. Weekly and monthly

**Weekly**, on a fixed day:

1. One row per piece published that week.
2. One sentence in the learning field.
3. Reply debt: comments from the last posts that never got an answer.

**Every two weeks:**

1. The three strongest and the three weakest pieces.
2. Ask which one variable differs: hook, pillar, format, or timing. Write the pattern into the ideas backlog.
3. Change one variable on the next piece. A piece that changes hook, format, and topic at once teaches nothing.

**Monthly:**

1. Compare the pillar mix with the proportions in `estrategia/01`. If `01` has no proportions yet, write them there during onboarding's corrections, or at this review with the creator.
2. A pillar at least 20% above the month's average on saves, sends, or watch time earns 5–10 points more of next month's mix, taken from a weaker pillar.
3. A pillar at least 20% below for two months in a row is not deleted. Authority takes time. Try a different format inside that pillar before shrinking it.
4. A format that keeps winning inside one pillar becomes the default for that pillar. The other format stays as an occasional test.

## 4. What a metric means for the next piece

| What you see | What changes next |
|---|---|
| Drop in the first seconds, or hook rate under this account's average | The first sentence and the first frame. See the hook rule. |
| Strong open, weak hold | Cut the stall before the proof. The open over-promised. |
| Saves and sends above average | Same angle, new hook (an iteration, not a new concept). |
| Reach with almost no follows | The piece entertained and did not give a reason to stay. Check the promise in `estrategia/01`. |
| Follows with weak saves | The person stayed for the creator and did not get a tool they can reuse. Add a concrete step. |
| A funnel stage over its share for a month | Rebalance at the idea stage, before writing. Direct promotion stays near the cap in `estrategia/07-funil-de-conteudo.md`. |

Do not boost a piece because a single like count looked friendly. Paid amplification follows `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`.

## 5. The loop back into ideas

A pattern that shows up twice becomes an idea or an edit on an existing idea. "Question hooks beat statements on this account" is a note on the next scripts, not a universal law. Confirm it on the next two pieces before writing it into `estrategia/10-copywriting.md`.

The pre-publish checklist stays `memory/checklist-revisao-pre-publicacao.md`. Metrics do not replace the creator's approval.
