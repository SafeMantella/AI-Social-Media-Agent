# 30-day cold-start sprint

An execution plan with a deadline. The living research stays in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`. When the 30 days end, this file becomes a record of what was tried. Do not keep editing it as if the sprint were still running. Write the result into the metrics log and into memory if a correction is reusable.

The plan borrows the useful half of a public 30-day personal-brand sprint: a swipe file of openers, a daily publish muscle, a single checkpoint, and comments answered fast. It drops the half that fights this system: copying a viral video's colors and timing, posting on one platform by deleting the others, chasing a follower count, and suppressing the creator's own proof for the first two weeks.

## 1. What to take, and what to leave

Take:

1. **A swipe file of openers.** Fifteen to twenty openings from short videos in this niche: the on-screen line and the first spoken sentence. Source them with the research tools that `memory/config.md` set to yes. Store them in `referencia/swipe-openers.md`. The pipeline rewrites the structure into this creator's pain. It does not paste the line.
2. **Two production modes, chosen by the subject.** Dense is the default: tight cuts the creator makes, screen recordings, on-screen text, the video pipeline. Use it when the lesson is the point. Light is a simple setup and a fast edit, for opinion, reaction, and behind the scenes. Choose by the subject. Do not choose light because the day is short. This template has no built-in light series (`memory/feedback-repo-do-dia-format.md`).
3. **Remix unlocks a small bank.** The opener comes from the swipe file. The substance is the creator's. That is the answer to "I cannot post until the bank is full."
4. **A weekly list of small tests.** About fifteen experiments under 30 minutes, dropped into the ideas backlog. Five minutes to list them. They feed behind-the-scenes and proof. They are not a second content strategy.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first two hours.**
6. **After the sprint, write to the five people who engaged most.** Ask where they are stuck on the topic in `estrategia/01`. That is product research.

Leave:

1. **Copying the source video's type, color, and timing.** Copy the verbal shape only. The visual system stays `estrategia/00-identidade-visual.md`.
2. **Quitting every platform except one.** During these 30 days, production energy goes to the one or two short-video platforms the creator names at the start. The others on `memory/social_media_handles.md` get repurposed pieces (`memory/content-format-cascade.md`). They do not get a separate daily job.
3. **Follower count as the score.** The score is pieces shipped, watch and completion, saves, sends, and whether a stranger could tell the niche from the grid. The goal in `estrategia/01` still wins if it says authority rather than reach.
4. **A long stretch of pure remix before any original proof.** Run remix and the creator's own work in the same week, from day one. The proof is the reason the account exists.

## 2. Before day 1

A prep week is allowed. It is about one week, and the start date is fixed before the prep begins. If prep slips, start on that date with what is ready.

- Bio on the sprint platforms matches `estrategia/01` and `memory/social_media_handles.md`. Audit: `estrategia/08-crescimento-e-auditoria-perfil.md`.
- Swipe file has its first openers. Empty is not ready.
- Buffer: about 6–8 short videos, 2–3 carousels, and one or two stories that are real. Enough for the first two weeks, not for the month.
- One dense piece and one light piece made during prep, so the effort is known before the clock starts.
- The metrics home is ready to receive a row on publish day (`estrategia/04-metricas-e-otimizacao.md`).

## 3. The floor for 30 days

- **4 short videos a week** on the sprint platforms, same file where the platforms accept it. Dense or light by subject. If four would hollow out the dense edit, drop to **3**. Do not thin the cut to protect the count.
- **2 carousels a week.** They give a visitor a reason to follow and something to save.
- **Stories** on days with a real behind-the-scenes moment. Skip the day rather than invent one.
- **The creator's own work in public** is one of those short videos each week, not a side project that starts on day 25.
- **Engagement:** about 20 minutes a day on a short list of accounts in the niche, plus replies on the new post inside two hours.

Mix the month across the pillars in `estrategia/01`. Do not invent a series to fill the slots. If a light series already exists because the creator asked for one through the feedback loop, it can occupy some of the light slots. It does not get the whole week. Leave at least one dense teaching piece each week. If the day-20 checkpoint shows that the dense pillar is what retains and the light pieces do not, rebalance before day 30.

### Each short video

1. Opener from the swipe file, rewritten for a specific pain. The hook rule still applies: one short sentence, no unexplained jargon.
2. Substance from a real pillar, with proof the creator can show.
3. On-screen text. Sound-off is the common case. Spoken captions stay within the video pipeline (at most 5 words a cue).
4. The video opens. The post caption carries the saveable detail: the steps, the definition, the link. The first 125 characters still work alone (`memory/checklist-revisao-pre-publicacao.md`). A carousel caption stays 1 to 3 sentences, because the slides carry the lesson.
5. One call to action. A save or a send by default. The comment word only when `dm_automation` is `yes`, and only the word already stored in `memory/dm-automation.md`. Do not invent a sample word in the script.

## 4. The only checkpoint

Around **day 20**, open the metrics log.

- Find the one to three short videos that beat the others.
- Days 20–30: more of that format, that pillar, and that opener shape.
- If nothing pulled ahead, the opener is the problem. Go back to the swipe file, match the structure more tightly, and shorten to one sentence.

## 5. What day 30 is allowed to look like

A modest number of new followers, one to three pieces that clearly beat the rest, and a few people who comment more than once. Compounding shows up around month three. The sprint exists so the creator does not quit in the phase that feels flat.

After day 30:

- Keep the floor. Bias the mix toward the checkpoint winners.
- Write to the five most engaged people. Ask where they are stuck.
- While the goal is growth or authority, do not introduce a paid offer in this window. A useful marker is about day 60, and only after the traffic prerequisites are true. If `estrategia/01` already says the goal is sales, do not pretend otherwise. Follow `memory/diretrizes-trafego.md` instead of this paragraph.

## 6. What this sprint does not change

- The short video stays the base format.
- The pipelines stay the order of work. Interview, then research, then the draft, then approval.
- The voice file still wins on tone.
- Nothing is published, built in Canva, or rendered at full resolution without an explicit yes.
