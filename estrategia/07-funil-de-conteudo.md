# Content funnel

When a piece is top, middle, or bottom, and how deep it should go. The thematic pillars live in `estrategia/01-posicionamento-e-pilares.md`. This file maps those pillars onto stages. It does not replace them.

Collected as a method from public funnel writing (including notes aimed at technical audiences) and from the usual top / middle / bottom split. The split was built for a product. A creator with an audience has to bend it. Re-check the sources when this file is older than two or three months if a decision depends on a channel claim.

## 1. Why the textbook funnel is not enough

Three failure modes show up for a creator:

- **Too much bottom.** The feed becomes a pitch. People who can smell a pitch stop trusting the teaching.
- **Too much top.** The piece is so broad it helps no one in the audience from `estrategia/01`. It can get views and still build no trust.
- **The middle is the hard, valuable part.** It teaches the problem and how to get through it, without naming a fee. That is what makes a later offer believable.

A useful shorthand: the top sparks curiosity, the middle builds confidence, the bottom gives a reason to act. On this system the bottom stays rare until the goal in `estrategia/01` is leads or sales.

## 2. Map stages to the pillars you already have

Do this once, with the creator, after onboarding, and write the result into `estrategia/01` or into the ideas backlog. A pillar can sit mostly in one stage.

| Stage | Job | Typical pillar job |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Reach people who are not in the inner circle yet. Light, specific, curious. | The pillar that travels outside the core niche |
| Middle | Teach how something works, or how the creator thinks, with no pitch | The core teaching pillars. Most pieces live here |
| Across | Keep a relationship at any stage | Community, culture, a real behind-the-scenes moment |
| Bottom | Name the offer, the service, or the paid next step | One commercial pillar, capped |

The functional layer (authority, connection, proof) in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md` cuts across these stages. A middle piece can still be proof. A top piece can still be authority. Do not collapse the two layers into one list.

**Classify an idea before writing:**

- It teaches, with no pitch → middle.
- It is light and built to reach past the current audience → top.
- It names an offer, a fee, or "hire me" → bottom, and it counts toward the cap.

## 3. How deep, and in what format

| Stage | Depth | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Top | A person outside the niche can follow it. One idea. | Short video, a light carousel, a before and after they can see |
| Middle | The real depth. Specialist terms are allowed after the hook has earned them. | The short video still opens it. Then a carousel, a thread, a long post, or a newsletter |
| Bottom | Light on jargon. Heavy on a concrete case the creator has permission to show. | A case, a delivery story, one discreet ask. The bio and the list footer carry the standing link |

The short video remains the base format even for a middle idea (`memory/content-format-cascade.md`). Depth that does not fit the video goes to the derivative, not into a slower open.

## 4. Channels

Use the platforms in `memory/social_media_handles.md`. Do not add a platform here that onboarding did not choose.

| Stage | Where it usually goes |
|---|---|
| Top | The short-video platforms on the list, plus a short text post |
| Middle | A saveable format: carousel, thread, long post, newsletter, a longer video if that platform is on the list |
| Bottom | The footer of the list, the link in the bio, a single ask at the end of a piece |

During a cold-start sprint, production energy stays on the one or two platforms named in `estrategia/09-sprint-cold-start-30-dias.md`. Other platforms on the list receive repurposed pieces. They do not get their own research project that week.

## 5. Proportion

No public source hands a creator a universal percentage. Use this as the default, then write the real mix into `estrategia/01` when the pillars exist:

- Most of the month is middle (teaching).
- A visible slice is top (reach) and connection.
- Bottom stays at or under about **one piece in five** while trust is still the goal.
- While `Goal` is growth or authority, bottom can be zero. The bio can still say what the creator does.

When `Goal` is sales, the bottom can rise. Cold pieces still speak as cold (`memory/diretrizes-trafego.md`). A month that is half bottom gets called out at the monthly review.

## 6. Daily use

When an idea is added to the backlog, answer two questions and store the answers on the idea:

1. Stage: teach, reach, or offer?
2. If it is an offer: have four non-offer pieces shipped since the last one? If not, it waits.

If the store can hold a select field, add **Funnel stage** (top / middle / bottom) on ideas and on ready pieces. A view that shows the month's mix is the alert. Do not create the field in a Notion database during a writing session without the creator's yes. In markdown, put the stage in the idea file's first lines and in `conteudo/metricas.md`.
