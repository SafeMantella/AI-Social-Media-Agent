# Growth and profile audit

How to audit the live profiles and what to keep true. This file holds no follower counts. Current numbers live in the metrics log from `estrategia/04-metricas-e-otimizacao.md`. Ranking research lives in `memory/growth_sales_critical_lens.md`. Use that file when a weight matters. Re-audit the profile when the positioning changes, before any paid spend, and at the start of the 30-day sprint.

The goal in `estrategia/01-posicionamento-e-pilares.md` decides what "growth" is for. A larger audience is the path. It is not a substitute for the promise.

## 1. Audit the live profile

Do this on every platform in `memory/social_media_handles.md`. If `referencia/perfil-atual/RESUMO.md` already exists, start from that note and re-open the pages. Write the findings in the chat and, if the creator agrees, fix the profile in the same session. Do not paste the old bio into this file.

### Bio

The bio states what the creator publishes, who it is for, and why a stranger would follow. A list of credentials with no promise fails that test. The source text is the promise, the niche, and the audience in `estrategia/01`, shortened until it fits the platform. If the live bio and `memory/social_media_handles.md` disagree, the live one is stale or the file is. Ask which one wins, then make them match.

### Follow ratio

People glance at followers versus following before they follow back, and so do brands and collaborators. It is a credibility cue, not a documented ranking weight. Move toward more followers than accounts followed before paid spend or a partnership pitch. Unfollow accounts that never reply and never inform the work. Do not wipe the following list to chase a ratio.

### Grid and highlights

The newest posts should be recognizable as the pillars in `estrategia/01` and the visual system in `estrategia/00-identidade-visual.md`. Old personal posts can stay in the archive. Highlights that contradict the promise should not be the first impression. Archive them. Do not delete them as part of this audit unless the creator asks.

A small account of real people is a seed, not a stain. Do not reset the account to "start clean" unless the creator decides the old grid actively repels the audience in `estrategia/01`.

### Picture

The avatar matches the visual system. When `face` is `yes`, a clear face is the default. When `face` is `no`, use the mark from `estrategia/marca/` if one exists. Do not generate a fake portrait.

## 2. Growth moves that survive a re-check

These are the applications of the ranking lens. If that file's dates are older than two or three months, re-check before treating a multiplier as fact.

1. **Total watch time, including replays, outweighs a 3-second view.** Open on the promise. Cut the slow introduction. The creator cuts the footage. The agent does not invent cuts.
2. **On-screen text in the first glance.** Sound-off is the common case. The spoken hook reinforces the text when `face` is `yes`. It does not replace the text.
3. **Sends outweigh likes for non-followers.** Test "send this to someone who has this problem" on teaching pieces. One ask only.
4. **A reply to a public piece in the niche** can earn attention. Use it when the creator has a real disagreement or a real addition. One or two a month is plenty. Do not build a calendar of empty stitches.
5. **Cadence:** 3–4 short videos a week aimed at non-followers, and 1–2 carousels a week that give a visitor a reason to follow. This matches the floor in `estrategia/01` if one was written there. If it was not, write the floor during the sprint setup.
6. **Sound-off, again.** Anything spoken that the viewer must understand is also on screen. On-screen captions stay at or under 5 words (`memory/feedback-video-editing-pipeline.md`).
7. **Reply to comments in the first two hours** after publishing. That is the window where conversation depth is cheapest to earn. Then the daily twenty minutes on other accounts in the niche.

## 3. Monetization, in order

A larger, more responsive audience is the prerequisite for a later offer. Indirect income (a service, a digital product, a teaching offer) usually matters more than a platform's ad share for a solo creator. That is a reason to get retention right before a louder commercial call to action. It is not permission to hide the promise. The bio can say what the creator does while the posts stay in the middle of the funnel (`estrategia/07-funil-de-conteudo.md`).

Do not skip from an empty grid to a sales page. The traffic guide's prerequisites still apply.

## 4. Standing checklist

- Bio matches `estrategia/01` and `memory/social_media_handles.md`.
- Follow ratio is moving the right way before paid spend or a partnership.
- The newest posts match the visual system and the pillars. The first post of a new phase is the stranger's first impression.
- `referencia/swipe-openers.md` holds openers from the niche: the on-screen title and the first spoken sentence, rewritten later into this creator's pain. Do not copy the source video's colors, timing, or type.
- After publishing: replies in the first two hours. One send-style ask on teaching pieces when it fits. The comment word, when used, is the one already in `memory/dm-automation.md`.
- A timely reply to someone else's public piece may jump the queue. Record that as a calendar decision in the store. Do not silently reorder a week of teaching pieces.
